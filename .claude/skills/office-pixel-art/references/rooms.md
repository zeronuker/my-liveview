# What each room contains

A plan for how much detail each room gets. Tick props off as they are built. A room feels finished when it has big shapes, mid-size furniture and small stories (steam, papers, a cat on a sill). Tutorials cover almost none of these props: draw them ourselves and check by eye.

The reference picture the user described (pixel-agent-desk's floor plan) is a guide for layout only; its art is not to be copied.

## Outside: the garden

- Grass all round with 3 or 4 variants, flower patches, paths of soil or stone, a wooden fence.
- Trees (pines and round trees), bushes, a few rocks.
- Animals that wander on their own: cats, dogs, chickens.
- Terrace by the front door: wood floor, flower pots, two armchairs, small tables with chairs.

## Reception and hall

- The entrance with a mat, a coat stand, a plant, a reception counter. Wide staircase with a landing, steps, railings.

## File room

- Shelves of files and boxes down both walls, a ladder, a trolley, a label board, a lamp.

## Lounge with pantry

- Sofas and armchairs on a rug, a coffee table, a bookshelf, a TV unit, plants, a lamp with a light pool.
- Pantry: counter, sink, coffee machine, fridge, kettle, mugs, a bin, a small table. Idle workers go here.

## Open-plan desks

- Desks in islands with a monitor or laptop each, chairs, papers, a mug, a small plant on some. Hanging lamps, a checkered carpet, a glass wall along the front.

## Meeting room

- A big table with 8 chairs, a TV, a whiteboard, a bookshelf, a plant, dark carpet. Helpers and plan-mode scenes happen here.

## Printer room

- Printer, boxes, a bin, a shredder, a radiator, a shelf with paper.

## Manager's office

- Desk with the tray, chair, a filing cabinet, a plant, a framed picture, a small shelf.

## Test lab

- The test machine, benches, tools on pegboard, a status board with red and green lights, tile floor, a small sink.

## CEO's office (upstairs)

- Big desk, leather chair, rug, bookshelf, a window with a view, a lamp, a plant, framed pictures.

## Everywhere

- Real walls, doorways with frames, windows, hanging lamps, rugs, plants, boxes, pictures on walls.
